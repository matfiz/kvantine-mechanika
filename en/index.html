Quantum Mechanics

We start with finite systems, apply their rules to particles in space, and examine the assumptions behind quantum states and measurements.

Part I — Finite Systems

We begin with finite systems, from the qubit to several systems taken together. Part II applies the rules to particles in a box, on the line and in space, and Part III examines the assumptions behind the rules.
