  1. Exercise 1

    Let CCC be conjugation in the σz\sigma_zσz​ basis. Compute CσjC−1C\sigma_jC^{-1}Cσj​C−1 and its action on a Bloch vector. Check directly that C(iψ)=−iCψC(i\psi)=-iC\psiC(iψ)=−iCψ and that ray transition probabilities are preserved.

    1. Hint

      Conjugate the Pauli matrix entries in the given basis. For the action on vectors, conjugate their coordinates and track the norm and the modulus of the overlap.

    Answer

    Conjugation fixes σx\sigma_xσx​ and σz\sigma_zσz​ and sends σy\sigma_yσy​ to −σy-\sigma_y−σy​. It sends the Bloch vector to (rx,−ry,rz)(r_x,-r_y,r_z)(rx​,−ry​,rz​). The map satisfies C(iψ)=−iCψC(i\psi)=-iC\psiC(iψ)=−iCψ and preserves ray transition probabilities.
